Parent & Teacher Setup Guide

Everything you need to set up a calm, tactile learning station in less than 5 minutes.

1. The Hardware Setup (Low Cost & Accessible)

You do not need an expensive tablet or dedicated laptop for your child. All you need is:

  • A Screen: Any Android TV, Google TV device (Chromecast, Xiaomi, Sony, OnePlus, TCL), or Android tablet.
  • A Physical Keyboard: Any standard USB wired keyboard (or Bluetooth keyboard). Plug it directly into the USB port of your TV or TV box.

Why wired? A $10–$15 wired keyboard has zero battery anxiety, zero Bluetooth pairing headaches, and provides real physical key travel that gives young children tactile muscle feedback.

2. The Card Launcher & Navigation

When you open the app, it presents two clean, high-contrast tactile cards:

  • Card 1: Level 1 (Free Typing) — Highlighted by default. Press Enter or Space to begin.
  • Card 2: Settings (Voice & Preferences) — Adjust speech pace, voice pitch, regional accent, and USB permissions.
  • Direct Typing Bypass for Young Children: If your child sits down and strikes any letter key directly on the menu, the app instantly enters Level 1 and types that letter. There is zero menu trapping!

3. Setting Up Real-World Photos (The Zero-DB Engine)

Nani Typing does not use cartoon clip art. Research shows young children anchor language far more effectively when connected to their actual environment: real family members, their own pets, familiar household items, and street scenes.

Step-by-Step USB Setup:

  1. Insert any standard USB thumb drive into your computer or phone.
  2. Create a folder named: NaniTyping (Case-insensitive: nanityping, NANITYPING all work).
  3. Drop your photos into this folder. Name each photo after the word you want to trigger:
    • kodi.webp → Displays when your child types KODI
    • hen.png → Displays when your child types HEN
    • mamma.jpg → Displays when your child types MAMMA
    • car.webp → Displays when your child types CAR
  4. Subfolders are supported! You can organize photos into categorized subfolders up to 3 levels deep:
    • NaniTyping/Family/mamma.jpg
    • NaniTyping/Vehicles/car.webp
    • NaniTyping/Animals/hen.png
  5. Plug the USB drive into your Android TV and launch Nani Typing.
  6. One-Time Android TV USB Permission: In Nani Typing, open the Settings card, select USB Drive Access, and press Enter. Toggle Allow on the Android system screen (this enables All Files Access, exactly like VLC), then press Back. Your photo count will update immediately!

4. Universal Multilingual & Dialect Support

Because the trigger is simply the filename, Nani Typing has zero regional language barriers:

  • Telugu: Name a photo of a dog kukka.jpg.
  • Hindi: Name the same photo kutta.jpg.
  • Tamil: Name the same photo naai.jpg.
  • English: Name the same photo dog.jpg.

Matching is case-insensitive. Typing DOG, dog, or Dog instantly matches dog.jpg.

5. Spacebar vs. Enter Key Mechanics

  • Letter Keys: Speaks uppercase letter phonics immediately upon pressing.
  • Spacebar (Word Phonics Loop): Spells completed word letter-by-letter with orange magnification ($1.5\times$), speaks whole word aloud, flashes real photo or kinetic action, and adds a space.
  • Enter Key (Sentence Synthesis): Spells word (if mid-word), pronounces word, and then reads all words in the line together as a fluent, continuous sentence before moving to a new line.
  • Backspace (Del / Forward Del): Erases last letter and immediately mutes ongoing audio.
  • Escape / Back: If text is present, resets canvas with *"Reset"*. If canvas is empty, returns to the Main Menu.

6. Kinetic Action Words Reference (Physics Play)

When your child types active verbs matching his physical play, the TV screen simulates real physical dynamics with a minimalist tactile vehicle:

  • FAST, SPEED, RUN, ZOOM: Accelerates rapidly across the screen ($450\text{ms}$) with realistic linear velocity.
  • SLOW, CRAWL: Creeps across the canvas in a measured $3.5\text{s}$ crawl.
  • STOP, HALT, WAIT: Screeches to an immediate halt with braking suspension tilt and recoil.
  • DOWN, DROP, FALL: Renders a structural ramp incline and accelerates downward under simulated gravity.
  • UP, CLIMB: Powers uphill against gravitational resistance.
  • BOUNCE, HOP: Free-fall drop with elastic spring rebound and squash-and-stretch.
  • JUMP: Smooth parabolic upward leap and return to ground.

7. Tips for Parents: The Montessori Flow

Do not force your child to sit for long sessions or complete structured flashcard drills. Allow him or her to type freely for 2 to 3 minutes whenever curiosity strikes. When children are free to lead their own exploration without adult interruption, their intrinsic love for learning thrives naturally.
