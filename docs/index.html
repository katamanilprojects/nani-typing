Treating Children Like Engineers, Not Passive Consumers

Why we completely rejected standard educational apps for our LKG son.

"He builds complex physical ramps from doormats and pillows to test gravity and velocity with his toy cars. He hated sitting still for adult-imposed flashcards, but loved mimicking us typing on our laptops. So we gave him a real wired keyboard, hooked it to our TV, and let him lead."

— Father & Creator of Nani Typing
🧠

Zero Over-Stimulation

No flashing stars, no cartoon fanfare, and no hyperactive sounds. A warm cream background, high-contrast navy lettering, and crisp phonetic feedback protect your child's natural attention span.

⌨️

Physical Sensory Feedback

Tapping glass screens is devoid of tactile resistance. A physical mechanical keyboard gives real physical feedback, developing fine-motor control, finger autonomy, and muscle memory.

🕊️

Child-Led Agency

No rigid levels to unlock. No timers or punishment for mistakes. He types freely for 2–3 minutes at a time, exploring phonics and language entirely on his own organic terms.

The 3 Progressive Levels

A developmental engine designed to evolve with your child from preschool through primary school.

🔤

Level 1: Phonics & Sentence Reading

Pressing any key speaks the letter name aloud. Spacebar loops back through the word, magnifying each letter in vibrant orange, then pronounces the whole word. Enter reads all words in the line together as a full fluent sentence.

📸

Level 2: Real-World Association

No clip art. When your child types a matching word, a real photograph of his actual surroundings (family members, pets, household items) flashes on screen. Dual-engine USB indexer supports subfolders like Family/ or Vehicles/.

🏎️

Level 3: Kinetic Physics Logic

Typing action words like FAST, SLOW, STOP, DOWN, UP, BOUNCE, or ZOOM triggers real physical kinetic movements with a minimalist architectural vehicle.

Tactile Card Launcher & TV Preferences

Designed for TV remote D-pads and physical keyboards with zero child friction.

🎴

Direct-Typing Card Launcher

The app boots into two clear, large tactile cards: Level 1 (Free Typing) and Settings (Voice & Preferences).

  • Arrow Keys (← →): Effortlessly switch card selection.
  • Enter / Space: Launches the selected card.
  • Direct-Typing Bypass: If your child sits down and strikes any letter directly on the menu, Level 1 immediately launches and types that character with zero menu hindrance.
⚙️

In-App TV Settings

Customize audio pace and storage directly on the TV without touching configuration files:

  • Speech Speed: Child Pace (0.70x), Very Slow (0.55x), or Normal (1.00x).
  • Voice Pitch: Child-friendly pitch (1.12x) or standard (1.0x).
  • Regional Accents: Indian English (en-IN), US English (en-US), UK English (en-GB).
  • USB Drive Access: One-click system permission toggle for Android TV Scoped Storage.

Zero Database.
Filename-as-the-Trigger.

Built for government schools, rural computer labs, and busy parents. Nani Typing has no complex configuration or databases.

  • Plug-and-Play USB: Simply drop your photos onto any USB drive in a NaniTyping folder.
  • Subfolder Organization: Organize images into folders like Family/ or Animals/ up to 3 levels deep.
  • Universal Multilingual: Want to teach Telugu? Name the photo kodi.png. For English, name it hen.png. Works instantly in any language.
