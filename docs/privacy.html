Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026 • Last Updated: October 8, 2026

Nani Typing ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source, non-profit educational application designed for young children learning phonics and typing. We believe deeply in protecting the privacy, attention, and innocence of children.

Our privacy policy is simple: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information whatsoever. The application is 100% offline.

1. Google Play Families Policy & COPPA Compliance

Nani Typing is designed specifically for children under the age of 13 and strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Developer Families Policy.

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store names, email addresses, birthdays, phone numbers, or geographic locations.
  • No Account Creation: The app does not require logins, accounts, profiles, or authentication of any kind.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app contains zero analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Google Analytics).
  • No Advertising: There are zero commercial advertisements, popups, banner ads, or in-app purchases.

2. Application Permissions & Network Isolation

You can inspect the application package (APK) or source code at any time to verify our permissions:

  • No Internet Permission: The app does NOT declare android.permission.INTERNET. The application is physically incapable of connecting to any server, website, or cloud service over Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or mobile data.
  • Local Storage Permissions (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): These permissions are requested strictly to allow Android TV users to plug in a USB flash drive or specify a local directory containing real-world family photos (e.g., mamma.jpg, hen.png). All photo indexing, downsampling, and rendering occur entirely in on-device local memory. No file data is ever modified, uploaded, copied, or transmitted off the device.
  • Microphone & Camera: The app does not request or access the device camera or microphone.

3. Device Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Spoken letter names, words, and sentences are synthesized locally using the device's default Android Text-to-Speech engine. No voice recordings or audio inputs are generated, recorded, or transmitted.

4. Data Security

Because all application state and typing data reside solely in the device's volatile memory (RAM) and are permanently erased when the app is closed or reset, no user data exists to be compromised or breached.

5. Changes to This Policy

Should this privacy policy be updated in future releases, the revised policy will always be published on this URL. Because our core design philosophy is strictly offline and local-first, our commitment to zero data collection will never change.
